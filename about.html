Our Story

About Puppy Dog Pals

Passionate about connecting dogs with loving homes across Botswana.

How It Started

From a Simple Love for Dogs

Puppy Dog Pals began in 2022 when our founder, after struggling to find a reputable breeder and quality pet products in Botswana, decided to create the solution she wished existed. What started as a small passion project has grown into a trusted community of dog lovers.

We believe every dog deserves a loving home, and every pet parent deserves access to quality products, expert advice, and a supportive community. That belief drives everything we do.

Our Mission

To enrich the lives of dogs and their owners in Botswana by providing trusted breed matching, premium pet products, and expert care resources. We strive to be the bridge that connects every dog with their perfect forever home.

Our Vision

To become Botswana's most trusted and loved pet community, where every dog owner feels supported, informed, and inspired. We envision a future where no dog goes without love, care, and quality products.
